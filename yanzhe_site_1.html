Introduction

About Me

I am a PhD student in the Alex Gould Lab (Physiology and Metabolism Laboratory) at The Francis Crick Institute, London. My research investigates how metabolic signals regulate neuroblast proliferation during development.

Before coming to the Crick, I completed my undergraduate studies at Peking University, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from the School of Life Sciences (supervised by Prof Alan Jian Zhu) and a Bachelor of Economics from the National School of Development.

Outside the lab I am a musician — I play the pipa and zhongruan and have performed in large-scale national orchestra concerts. I find that music and science share a surprising amount: both reward patience, precision, and a willingness to sit with complexity.

Beyond the Lab

Music

Music has been as central to my life as science. During my time at Peking University I was a performer in the Chinese Music Institute (北京大学中乐学社), participating in multiple large-scale national orchestra concerts. I play the pipa and zhongruan, and have performed alongside Xinyue Ai in several major Chinese orchestral productions.
